# Introducing Velora UI: the free tier is the whole product

<PostMeta
  date="July 16, 2026"
  category="Announcements"
  readingTime="4 min read"
/>

Every animated component library follows the same playbook: give away the
primitives, sell the assembled result. The marquee is free; the landing page
built from marquees is $199.

Velora UI inverts that. The complete template — this blog, the pricing page,
the auth screens, the changelog, the 404, every section of the homepage — is
the free tier, MIT licensed, commercial use included. If your product needs
one great landing site, you never owe us anything.

## What's in the box

- **32+ animated components**, from the signature aurora background to
  border beams, animated lists, docks, terminals and device mockups.
- **A complete multi-page template** you can clone and rename: home, pricing,
  blog with an MDX pipeline, about, contact, login, signup, changelog and a
  custom 404.
- **A standard shadcn registry**, so every component installs with one
  command into your own project:

```bash
npx shadcn@latest add https://velora.colorlib.com/r/aurora-background.json
```

Components carry their own keyframes and design tokens, so they work in any
shadcn project — Base UI or Radix, existing design system or fresh scaffold.

## The three rules every component follows

**It reads your tokens.** No hardcoded hex values. Change the brand variables
once and every gradient, beam and glow follows.

**It respects reduced motion.** A global kill switch disables every
animation when the visitor asks for less movement — not as a per-component
afterthought, but as a design constraint from day one.

**It publishes its cost.** Every docs page shows the component's gzipped
size and dependency count. Animation is a budget line, and you deserve to see
it before you install.

## What Pro will be — and won't be

Pro (coming later this year, $99 one-time) adds breadth: more niche
templates, section variants, Figma files and integration wiring. It will
never claw back anything that's free today. The free tier isn't a funnel —
it's the product. Pro is for teams who want shortcuts.
